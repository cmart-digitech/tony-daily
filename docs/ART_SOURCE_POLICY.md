# Art Source Policy

## Sources (verified live 13 Aug 2026)
The Art Newspaper, ArtAsiaPacific, Artnet News, Hyperallergic — all via
their public RSS feeds, all Tier C specialist editorial. Same rules as every
other source: verified before commit, polite cadence, metadata + permitted
excerpt only, publisher one click away.

## Auction data (brief §44–45) — deliberately not built yet
Structured AuctionEvent/AuctionLot entities require a legitimate structured
source. The major houses (Christie's, Sotheby's, Phillips, Bonhams) do not
expose public feeds for sale calendars, and scraping their sites — let alone
authenticated bidding systems — is prohibited by the product rules. Until a
legitimate feed/API/licence exists: auction news arrives through the art
publications above, and **no estimate, bid, hammer price or sale date is
ever displayed except inside a sourced article**. Nothing is inferred,
converted or invented.

## Trending (brief §46) — evidence only
No unexplained popularity scores. If trending is added, each item must carry
its reason ("covered by N connected sources", "major Hong Kong sale this
week per <source>"). Frequency within the connected corpus is the only
automatic signal available today.

## Imagery (brief §48)
Feed/OG images from the publishers with attribution; the typographic
placeholder otherwise. Never an AI reproduction of a real artwork.
